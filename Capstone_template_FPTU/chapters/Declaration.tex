\chapter{Declaration of AI-Assisted Technologies during the Project}\label{chapter:decl}

During the preparation of this work, the authors employed AI tools to support various stages of the research and development process. We declare the use of these technologies as follows:

\paragraph{1. Writing and Editing Assistance}
Generative AI tools, including ChatGPT and Gemini, were used to improve the readability and flow of the academic report. Specifically, these tools assisted in refining grammatical structures, suggesting synonymous vocabulary for precision, and ensuring adherence to formal academic tone. However, the core scientific arguments, structural organization, and conclusions presented in this document are entirely the original work of the authors.

\paragraph{2. Coding and Technical Support}
AI-powered coding assistants, such as GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT, were utilized to accelerate the development of the codebase. These tools aided in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Supporting to build the Gradio web interface and data visualization modules.
    \item Debugging complex errors within the PyTorch training pipelines.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{3. Responsibility Statement}
While AI technologies facilitated the drafting and coding processes, the authors performed a comprehensive review of all AI-generated outputs. We verified the accuracy of the written content and the functional correctness of the code. The authors assume full responsibility for the final content of this publication, including the integrity of the data, the validity of the analysis, and the originality of the research findings.
